What just happened?
  1. You clicked "Continue with Google"
  2. We generated a secret PKCE verifier and stored it in your session
  3. We sent a hashed version of it (the challenge) to Auth0
  4. Auth0 showed you a Google login — your password went to Google, not us
  5. Google confirmed your identity to Auth0
  6. Auth0 redirected back here with a short-lived code
  7. We sent that code + the original verifier to Auth0's token endpoint
  8. Auth0 hashed our verifier and checked it matched the challenge
  9. Auth0 issued a signed ID token containing your name and email
  10. We verified the token's signature using Auth0's public keys (JWKS)
